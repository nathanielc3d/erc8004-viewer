ERC-8004 identity viewer

Reads an agent's on chain identity (owner, registration file) and its reputation feedback straight from the ERC-8004 registries on Base and Base Sepolia. No backend: your browser calls a public RPC.

Try: Base 1, Base Sepolia 1, Kognai test identity, an agent that does not exist. Theme: light, dark, system.
